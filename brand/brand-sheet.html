H-sib brand sheet

One mark for every H-sib app. The H stays. Each app adds one small thing and one accent color.

The rule

  1. The H never changes. Stems 7 wide, bar 6 tall, 10 between the stems, on a 24 by 24 grid.
  2. One small addition per app. A seam, a notch, a tick. Declared adds a stepped seam: the H is two halves of one token that only fit each other.
  3. One accent color per app. The changing half takes it. The other half stays silver.
  4. The plate stays. Dark navy rounded square, soft aura of the accent color, thin light rim, a little shadow under the mark.
